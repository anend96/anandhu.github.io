About Me

Exploring the Universe,
One Burst at a Time

I'm an astrophysics researcher and deep tech entrepreneur based in Padova, Italy. My work focuses on studying repeating Fast Radio Bursts — among the most enigmatic phenomena in the Universe — using radio telescopes like the Sardinia Radio Telescope, GMRT, and GBT.

I also founded Astralis Deep Tech Initiative, where I develop GNSS signal integrity tools using ML-based anomaly detection.

What I Do

Research Areas

Fast Radio Bursts

Studying high-frequency behaviour, polarimetry, and activity windows of repeating FRBs — particularly FRB 20180916B — with major radio telescopes worldwide including SRT, GMRT, and GBT.

GNSS Signal Integrity

Developing the Angulus Project — TRL 3 PoC achieved. ESA BIC Padua Incubation Candidate.

Compact Object Physics

Modelling post-supernova spatial distribution of black holes and neutron stars within Milky Way potentials using N-body simulations and SevnPy.

X-ray Astronomy

Multi-wavelength campaign design and analysis using AstroSAT and Chandra data to constrain FRB progenitor models and emission mechanisms.

ML for Astrophysics

Applying TensorFlow, PyTorch, and CuPy to accelerate data pipelines, classify radio transients, and build anomaly detection frameworks for space data.

Science Outreach

Founding the International Students Collective at UniPadova — awarded €5,000 (2024) and €4,000 (2025) for cultural and scientific outreach programmes.

My Work

Research Projects

📡
FRB · Publication

Multiwavelength Study of FRB 20180916B

Characterizing activity windows, chromaticity, and polarimetry of repeating FRB 20180916B. Results in preparation for publication. Supervisors: Dr. Pilia & Dr. Zampieri.

🛰️
GNSS · Deep Tech

Angulus — AI RAN GNSS intrigity

Mahalanobis distance-based anomaly detection for GNSS I/Q datasets. TRL 3 PoC. Multi-channel correlation strategies. ESA BIC Padua project.

💥
Compact Objects

Post-SN BH/NS Spatial Distribution

Post-supernova kick distribution of black holes and neutron stars within a Milky Way potential using SevnPy and mpi4py. Supervised by Dr. Giuliano Iorio.

🌌
FRB · High Freq

High-Freq Characterization FRB 20180916B

Activity constraints at higher frequencies using the Sardinia Radio Telescope. 51-hour PI proposal (SRT 11-24). Supervised by Dr. Maura Pilia at INAF Cagliari.

🔭
FRB · Optical

Searching for Optical Counterpart of FRBs

GBT 25B-189 proposal (21 hrs) searching for the elusive optical counterpart of repeating fast radio bursts. Co-I with international team.

📻
Polarimetry

Long-term Polarization Position Angle Study

GMRT 48-087 (48 hrs) — long-term monitoring of polarization position angles of periodically repeating FRB 20180916B.
